# Lanterns at the edge of the woods

In Eldoria, every road is measured by the lanterns still burning along it.
The people of Lanternfall keep theirs lit for travelers crossing the Whispering
Meadow, where old forest paths wind around cold blue water.

Ulf, the village blacksmith, repairs more than hinges and horseshoes. He keeps
names: every traveler who leaves a lantern on his doorstep becomes part of the
village's story. Beyond the meadow, a grey wolf follows the road at dusk.

This example defines places and their inhabitants. It does not define combat,
inventories, quests, or player state.
